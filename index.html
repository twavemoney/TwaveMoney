ABOUT T-WAVE COIN
T-WAVE COIN is a digital platform built around the T-WAVE ecosystem. Registered users receive a unique User ID and can view their T-WAVE balance and the current platform rate.

Purchases, withdrawals and account support are handled through the T-WAVE support chat — tap the chat button any time.
HOW T-WAVE WORKS
1
Buy coin

Tap Buy Coin, pay to the account shown, then confirm on WhatsApp. Once your payment is confirmed, your T-WAVE is added to your balance.

2
Check the rate from time to time

The rate at the top is live and updates automatically. Tap Convert to Naira to see what your balance is worth at the current rate.

3
Withdraw

When you decide to withdraw, tap Withdraw and send your request. You need a T-WAVE balance first, so buy coin if your account is empty.

The rate can go up or down. Nothing on this page is a guaranteed return.
